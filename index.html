The Grade-Separated PRT Paradigm

Challenging traditional Light Rail Transit (LRT) by introducing a minimalist, aerial point-to-point network that integrates seamlessly into existing urban corridors.

Rigid Composite Network

Continuous overhead rigid guide-conductor rail composites provide physical tracking constraints and continuous, real-time power supply, eliminating massive ground lines.

eVTOL Lift Offloading

Distributed Electric Propulsion (DEP) multi-rotor assembly dynamically generates aerodynamic lift to offload the track structure's dead load by up to 60%, enabling extremely slender supports.

Point-to-Point routing

Individual AeriRod pods bypass intermediate stops. Active mechanical branching switches the pods directly into destination stations, avoiding line congestion entirely.

Wayside Infrastructure

Track & Guide Infrastructure

Designed with utility and aesthetic simplicity, AeriRail replaces heavy concrete bridge girders with slender steel davit columns. Guide networks are installed over traffic lanes, mimicking historical trolley wire locations.

Cantilever Offset 1.0 m Lateral Distance

Conductor guide tracks are offset horizontally from support poles, maintaining safe clearances from wayside obstacles.

Anti-Interference Geometry Pole Apex ≤ Rail Height

Support columns are height-restricted. Their apex cannot extend past the conductor rail plane, ensuring that the 3.1 m wide rotor blades pass freely above support structures without collision risk.

Safety Buffer Zone Roadway Striping

High-visibility right-of-way paint markings on the roadway explicitly identify vehicle tracking zones below.
